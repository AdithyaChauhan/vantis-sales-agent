## Tuning notes

Written by hand after the tuning run on 2026-10-11. The script does not regenerate this section.

### What was wrong in T3, and what changed

For "Do you provide installation in Delhi?" the T3 hybrid search put KB Section 10 only 4th. Two causes:

1. `ts_rank_cd` has no IDF, so common words ("provide", "installation") outweigh the rare word "Delhi".
2. Keyword ties were ordered by chunk_id, so tied chunks got arbitrary ranks (that is how KB Section 1, Company Overview, kept showing up).

Three keyword-side fixes were tried, one at a time and then combined, on the same 24 questions with the same question vectors (one tuning run of 32 embedding calls; all variants are database work after that):

- **(a) Shared tie ranks.** Chunks with the same keyword score share a rank (like SQL `RANK()`: 1, 2, 2, 4) instead of being split by chunk_id.
- **(b) Common-word filter.** Drop query words found in more than X% of the searchable chunks. Document frequency comes from Postgres (`ts_stat` over the 48 non-policy chunks), is loaded once and cached. If every word would be dropped, the original query is kept. X = 40, 35, 30 and 25% were tried.
- **(c) Weighted RRF.** The keyword list counts 0.5 and the vector list 1 in the fusion.

A sanity check: the new query builder with all three fixes switched off reproduces the T3 hybrid lists for all 32 questions, and the Delhi question is 4th (hybrid) and 2nd (vector), as T3 reported.

### Picking the winner

The rule was: best hybrid hit@3, then hybrid MRR, and a smaller combination wins a tie.

- Twelve variants reach hybrid hit@3 = 22. Among them MRR is 0.907 with keyword weight 0.5 and 0.901 with weight 1.0.
- Tie handling (a) makes no difference once the filter is on: "split" and "shared" give the same numbers in every row with a cutoff of 35% or lower. **(a) was removed from the code.** On its own it is mixed (it fixed two questions and made one worse), and on top of (b) it adds nothing.
- **(b) is kept** and is the fix that matters: hybrid hit@3 goes from 20 to 22, the Delhi question from 4th to 1st, and keyword-only hit@3 from 18 to 20.
- **(c) is kept**, with a caveat. On top of (b) it moves only two questions, both below the top 4 that the agent sees: the product question q01 from 8th to 6th and the Pune question q09 from beyond 10th to 10th. That is MRR +0.006, which is noise on 24 questions. It stays because it wins by the stated rule, because on its own it is a real gain (hit@3 20 to 21), and because vector search is the stronger list on this KB (MRR 0.867 against 0.781), so giving it more weight fits the data. It costs one constant: set `KEYWORD_WEIGHT = 1.0` in `app/rag/retriever.py` to drop it.
- **Cutoff 30%.** 35%, 30% and 25% are indistinguishable here. 30% is the value suggested in the task and the middle of that flat range, so a few chunks more or less in the KB should not change which words are dropped. At 40% the gain disappears, because "installation" is in 37.5% of the chunks.

### Relevance gate

- Best cosine similarity: KB questions 0.676 to 0.837 (lowest q17, "Do you work with clinics and small offices?"), off-topic questions 0.492 to 0.576 (highest o08, "Tell me a joke."). The gap is 0.100 wide. The threshold 0.63 sits inside it, 0.046 below the lowest KB question and 0.054 above the highest off-topic one. **It stays at 0.63** (`RETRIEVAL_MIN_SIMILARITY`).
- Three of the eight off-topic questions do get keyword hits, because generic words exist in the KB: "India" (the cricket question; KB Section 7 says "North India"), "list" (the Python question; it matches "listed" in KB Sections 7 and 10), and "price" and "share" (the Reliance share price question; KB Sections 5, 1, 14 and 15). With the T3 rule ("any keyword hit counts as context") those three would have passed the gate.
- No KB question needs its keyword hits to pass (all 24 are above 0.63 on similarity alone). So **the rule changed**: when a vector search ran, only its best similarity counts. Keyword hits count only in the keyword-only fallback, where there is no similarity (`has_context` in `app/rag/retriever.py`).
- Result: hybrid blocks 8 of 8 off-topic questions and 0 of 24 KB questions. In keyword-only mode it blocks 5 of 8 off-topic questions (cricket, Python and share price pass on a word match) and wrongly blocks 1 of 24 KB questions (q22, Gurgaon, no keyword hits). That is accepted for a fallback that has only words to go on. The answer prompt (T5) must still refuse off-topic requests whatever the gate says.

### Caveats

- The same 24 questions were used to tune and to report, and there is no held-out set. One question is 4.2 points of hit@k, so differences of one question between variants are noise, and numbers on new questions will probably be a little lower.
- The word statistics come from 48 chunks. A few chunks more or less can move a word across the cutoff, so re-run this eval when the KB changes.
- The word statistics are cached per process. After a re-ingest, restart the API so the filter sees the new counts.
- A full eval run costs 32 embedding calls (the free tier allows 1,000 a day). This task used two runs: one for tuning and one for this report.
